## Last Few Weeks (2026/06/29-2026/08/26)

## 1. Completed the Sparser MoE optimization stack

The 2,304-expert, top-36, EP72 path now preserves compact routing through TE, MCore, and HybridEP, transfers only destination-local probabilities, skips inactive experts during permutation, and uses tuned combine and bitset scan paths.

- **GB200:** 403.7 vs 125.3 TFLOP/s/GPU (**3.22x**).
- **GB300:** 419.7 vs 127.2 TFLOP/s/GPU (**3.30x**).

The TE router work ([#3012](https://github.com/NVIDIA/TransformerEngine/pull/3012), [#3129](https://github.com/NVIDIA/TransformerEngine/pull/3129)) and DeepEP dense scan ([#673](https://github.com/deepseek-ai/DeepEP/pull/673)) are merged. The focused MCore integration is in [#6614](https://github.com/NVIDIA/Megatron-LM/pull/6614) and [#6615](https://github.com/NVIDIA/Megatron-LM/pull/6615).

## 2. Added Kimi K3 MoE support across the GPU stack

The implementation covers K3's three MoE-specific mechanisms: SiTU-GLU, Quantile Balancing, and RMSNorm between routed aggregation and the latent up-projection. The [Kimi K3 MoE note](https://github.com/harryzhou2000/megatron-lm-moe-experiments/blob/945e8fe0091b315e7328111fff9877cab4eb65df/notes/kimi_k3_moe.md) explains the design and mathematics.

The lower-stack work is merged through cuDNN Frontend [#645](https://github.com/NVIDIA/cudnn-frontend/pull/645) and [#670](https://github.com/NVIDIA/cudnn-frontend/pull/670), and Transformer Engine [#3395](https://github.com/NVIDIA/TransformerEngine/pull/3395) and [#3402](https://github.com/NVIDIA/TransformerEngine/pull/3402). The MCore integrations are tracked in SiTU-GLU [#6673](https://github.com/NVIDIA/Megatron-LM/pull/6673)/[#6674](https://github.com/NVIDIA/Megatron-LM/pull/6674), Quantile Balancing [#6637](https://github.com/NVIDIA/Megatron-LM/pull/6637)/[#6638](https://github.com/NVIDIA/Megatron-LM/pull/6638), and latent RMSNorm [#6804](https://github.com/NVIDIA/Megatron-LM/pull/6804)/[#6803](https://github.com/NVIDIA/Megatron-LM/pull/6803).

## 3. Added variable-length Qwen3.5 mock-vision replay

A public JSONL format now replays empirical vision shapes without private metadata. It supports variable LLM sequence lengths, packed THD, padded BSHD, mixed vision/text batches, and text-only samples. The isolated dataloader work is in private fork-local draft PR [#1](https://github.com/harryzhou2000/Megatron-LM/pull/1).

## 4. Isolated MCore dense routing

Dense routing was separated from the experimental branch into focused router, dispatcher, configuration, and test changes. The path preserves compact `[T,K]` indices and padded `-1` routes, applies backend and dtype capability gates, and falls back to the existing bool route when unsupported. The `dev` and `main` PRs are [#6614](https://github.com/NVIDIA/Megatron-LM/pull/6614) and [#6615](https://github.com/NVIDIA/Megatron-LM/pull/6615).

## 5. Made the many-expert work reusable

The legacy Qwen3-Next Sparser MoE launcher was ported into readable model and recipe YAML, and a common GB200/GB300 matrix now covers the main many-expert workloads. The reusable MCore `moe_perf` recipe frontend is isolated in private fork-local draft PR [#2](https://github.com/harryzhou2000/Megatron-LM/pull/2).

The Sparser MoE work was also consolidated into one technical narrative covering the end-to-end workflow, matched performance, and the optimization paths that did not pay off.
